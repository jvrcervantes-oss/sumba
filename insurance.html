Insurance

Last updated: June 2026 · Managed by Bali Best Motorcycle

What's Included

Every rental includes a helmet at no extra charge. Basic third-party liability coverage is included as required by Indonesian law.

✓ Included

  • 1 helmet per bike
  • Basic third-party liability
  • 24/7 roadside assistance contact
  • Mechanical breakdown support

✗ Not included

  • Damage to the rented bike
  • Theft of the bike
  • Personal accident cover
  • Medical expenses

Full Damage Cover

Every booking includes one of two protection options, chosen and paid online at checkout:

  • Insurance Fee — 100,000 IDR per bike, per day. Non-refundable. Covers accidental damage to the bike for the duration of the rental.
  • Refundable Deposit — Rp 3,000,000 fixed per bike. Refunded after the bike is returned undamaged; deducted to cover repair costs otherwise.

What's Not Covered (any plan)

  • Damage caused by riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Damage resulting from participation in any race or speed test
  • Theft resulting from negligence (e.g. bike left unlocked)
  • Damage caused by an unlicensed rider
  • Intentional damage

⚠ We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance that includes motorbike riding before your trip. Check that your policy covers Southeast Asia and off-road terrain.

In Case of Accident

If you are involved in an accident:

  • Ensure personal safety first — move to a safe location if possible
  • Call emergency services: 112 (Indonesia national emergency)
  • Contact us immediately via WhatsApp: +62 881-0379-78255
  • Do not move the bike if police attendance is required
  • Document the scene with photos before any vehicle is moved
  • Obtain a police report (Surat Keterangan Kecelakaan) if third parties are involved
