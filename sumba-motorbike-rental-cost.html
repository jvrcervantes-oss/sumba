Why the Cheapest Bike Isn't the Cheapest Trip

Sumba is not Bali. Roads turn from tarmac to dirt to loose volcanic stone, there are long gaps between fuel stations, and a tubeless puncture with no compressor for 40 km is a genuinely ruined day. A 110cc plastic scooter saves you a couple of euros a day and costs you the trip the first time the road gets rough.

The BH-G3 is a custom aluminium trail scooter — 80 kg, spoke wheels, tube tyres you can fix anywhere, long-range LED lights for unlit roads. It is the difference between seeing the island and worrying about the bike. For two extra euros a day, that is the easiest decision of your trip.
