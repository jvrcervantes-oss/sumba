2. Data We Collect

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To answer your enquiry and quote a rental
  • To arrange delivery, hand-over and return of the motorbike
  • To take payment and keep a record of the transaction
  • To follow up on an enquiry you started, until you ask us to stop
  • To send you information about our rental plans, where you have consented
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations

4. WhatsApp and the Automated Assistant

Our WhatsApp number is answered by a mix of our team and an automated assistant. Both see the same conversation, and a member of our team can take over at any point.

In practical terms this means:

  • Your messages travel through the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.
  • The text of your conversation is sent to Anthropic PBC, which runs the Claude model that drafts the assistant's replies. Anthropic processes it on our instructions and does not use it to train its models.
  • Your conversation, contact details and enquiry are stored in our own customer system, hosted on Railway servers, so our team can pick the enquiry up.
  • A confirmed booking is also entered into our rental management system, which holds the booking record.

The assistant only handles rental enquiries for Bali Best Motorcycle. Please do not send card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information in chat. If you would rather speak to a person, just ask, and we will take over the conversation.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers that let us run the service:

  • Meta Platforms — WhatsApp message delivery
  • Anthropic PBC — the automated assistant's replies
  • Railway — hosting of our customer system
  • Stripe and Xendit — payment processing, when you pay online
  • Google — email and spreadsheet tools used by our team
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

Some of these providers are outside Indonesia, so your data may be transferred abroad and handled under the safeguards those providers apply.

6. How Long We Keep It

  • Enquiries that do not become a booking: up to 24 months from your last message
  • ID and licence photos: automatically deleted 60 days after we receive them
  • Booking and payment records: as long as Indonesian tax and accounting law requires

You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time. See our Data Deletion page.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for analytics and advertising. You can manage or block them through your browser settings.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, including Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27/2022) and, where it applies to you, the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have it corrected or deleted
  • Object to or restrict how we process it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Complain to your data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email balibestmotorcycle@gmail.com. We answer within 5 business days.
