About Lede Kalumbang Airport (TMC)

Lede Kalumbang is the main airport for West Sumba, with IATA code TMC. It's located near Tambolaka, about 42 km from Waikabubak (the main town in West Sumba). Flights operate daily from Bali (DPS), and several times a week from Lombok and Jakarta.

The arrivals area is small — one hall, one exit. There's no confusion about where to find us. When you step outside, we're there.

Why This Matters in Sumba

Sumba is not Bali. There are no Grab drivers waiting for notifications. There's no network of taxis with meters. Getting from one end of the island to the other requires planning — and ideally your own transport.

The highlights that make Sumba worth the trip are spread across the island: Weekuri Lake is 45 km from Tambolaka, Nihiwatu Beach is 65 km, and the traditional clan villages of Anakalang are 35 km. Without your own wheels, you're either paying for taxis every single day or missing half the island.

Getting a bike at the airport means your first two hours in Sumba are spent riding toward your first destination — not waiting for a driver who might not show up.
